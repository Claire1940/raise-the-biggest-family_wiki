Raise The Biggest Family how to get more kids is mainly a progression question: you need enough adoption capacity, enough coins, and a home that can support a larger household. Build a steady income first, then use your savings to add children in a controlled order.

<Section icon={Target} title="Child Care and Household Management">

New children may need a crib and a formula station at home. Place the required furniture early so you can respond to needs without unnecessary travel. When a bottle is unavailable, a timer may control when the next one can be prepared, so plan other tasks during that wait.

| Clothing | Dress the child in an outfit | Combine outfit tasks with task-panel objectives |

Travel can be part of the care routine. If a child wants a toy or another item, use your vehicle to visit the relevant store. The furniture store is one destination for household and child-related purchases. Compare your available coins with the item price before confirming the purchase.
