<Section icon={Apple} title="How to Spend Apples on Family Upgrades">

Apples are the central progression currency described for the experience. Efficient household development depends less on spending quickly and more on keeping enough Apples available for the next meaningful family action.

A useful rule is to separate required spending from optional spending. Required spending is tied to a displayed adoption condition, Family Go objective, or household action. Optional spending may be enjoyable, but it should not delay the next clear progression target.

| Apple Decision | Priority | Recommended Approach |
|---|---:|---|
| Next adoption requirement | High | Save until the displayed condition is met |
| Family Go objective | High | Confirm the objective and its Apple cost first |
| Resource-building action | Medium | Use when it supports the next household goal |
| Optional activity | Medium | Consider only after preserving progression resources |
| Unclear purchase | Low | Wait until its purpose is visible |

<Section icon={Shield} title="Upgrade Mistakes to Avoid and FAQ">

Household progression is easier when you avoid unsupported assumptions. The supplied information does not confirm a universal maximum family size, a complete house-level system, fixed milestone numbers, or a permanent building upgrade tree. Use current in-game prompts for those details.

Common mistakes include spending all Apples before checking adoption requirements, ignoring the household screen, and treating an activity as a house upgrade without confirming its effect. A disciplined review after every major action keeps your progression organized.

| Mistake | Likely Result | Better Habit |

| Ignoring requirements | Resources may be used inefficiently | Read the prompt first |
| Assuming house tiers | You may follow unsupported costs | Verify live upgrade labels |
| Skipping Family Go | Useful objectives may be missed | Review activities regularly |
| Not checking the roster | Progress can be difficult to track | Confirm every household change |
