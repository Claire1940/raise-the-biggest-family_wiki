<Section icon={Apple} title="Apple Farming and Spending Strategies">

Earn enough to support the next meaningful household action, then review the balance immediately after spending.

A good early routine is to identify the next adoption or family objective before beginning an earning session. This gives every Apple a purpose and reduces the chance of spending resources on an action that delays household growth.

| Strategy | Best Use | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Save for adoption | Expanding the household | High |
| Complete required activities | Advancing a visible objective | High |
| Maintain a reserve | Preparing for the next prompt | High |
| Spend on optional actions | Testing secondary features | Medium |
| Random spending | No clear progression goal | Low |

<Section icon={Target} title="Family Go Progression and Daily Priorities">

Family Go is a central system for organizing family activities, household objectives, and progression prompts. Open it regularly and inspect the available options before choosing an activity. The best choice depends on your current goal, the displayed requirement, and the effect on your household progress.

Use the following priority order when several actions are available:

1. Complete a required family objective.
2. Choose an activity connected to the next household-growth target.
3. Preserve enough Apples for a visible adoption or required action.
4. Recheck the roster and objectives after completing an activity.
5. Use optional activities only after core progression is secure.

| Progression Situation | Recommended Choice | Resource Approach |
|---|---|---|
| New player | Learn the Family Go interface | Avoid unnecessary spending |
| Next adoption is close | Prioritize the required earning loop | Save for adoption |
| Several activities are available | Select the activity tied to your goal | Compare displayed costs |
| Household recently expanded | Review the roster and new prompts | Rebuild your reserve |
| Objective is unclear | Inspect every visible requirement | Delay optional purchases |
