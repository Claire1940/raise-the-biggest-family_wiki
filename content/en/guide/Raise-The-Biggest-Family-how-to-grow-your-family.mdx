<Section icon={Trophy} title="Best Progression Habits and Common Mistakes">

Players usually progress more consistently when every action has a clear purpose. Build a simple routine around the next adoption, then adjust it when Family Go or the household interface presents a new objective.

| Habit | Effective approach | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Plan before spending | Check the next requirement first | Fewer resource shortages |
| Track the roster | Review household changes regularly | Clearer progress visibility |
| Use activities carefully | Choose objectives tied to current goals | Better Apple discipline |
| Repeat the core loop | Earn, adopt, review, and repeat | Steadier household growth |
| Verify new features | Follow live prompts and interface text | Less reliance on assumptions |

Common mistakes include:

- Spending all Apples before checking the next adoption requirement.
- Ignoring the household management screen after adding a member.
- Choosing an activity without reviewing its displayed cost.
- Assuming every adoption or milestone uses the same condition.
- Treating an unconfirmed maximum family size as a fixed target.
- Leaving Family Go without checking whether the objective changed.

The game supports offline growth for children, which means returning later can be part of the broader family routine. Even so, review the current household and available objectives when you return. Offline progression does not remove the need to plan Apples or verify the next adoption condition.
