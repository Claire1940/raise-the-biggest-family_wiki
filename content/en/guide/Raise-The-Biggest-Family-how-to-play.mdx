<Section icon={Baby} title="Raise The Biggest Family How to Play: Core Gameplay Overview">

Raise The Biggest Family is a Roblox life-simulation game where your goal is simple in name and layered in practice: grow the biggest family possible. You start by adopting your first baby at the adoption center, bring them home, and then juggle feeding, playing, dressing, and shopping while your income slowly builds. Every additional child costs more in adoption fees, so smart money management is the real skill behind the cute exterior.

The game rewards consistency. Coins generate every hour, daily logins stack up rewards, and task lists hand out cash for small chores like dressing your baby or buying a toy. Players who return each day and keep up with tasks expand their families far faster than those who binge once and disappear.

<Section icon={Map} title="Step-by-Step: Your First Adoption">

Getting your first child is the tutorial moment that sets the tone for the whole game. Follow this sequence to get home with your new kid quickly.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Go to the Adoption Center">
    Drive to the adoption center and get in line. Lines can be long, so be prepared to wait or pay to skip the queue if the option is available.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Answer the Interview">
    A staff member asks questions like whether this is your first child and whether you have a house with a yard. Answer honestly to proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Follow Staff to the Nursery">
    After being called, follow the staff member to the nursery and choose the baby you want to adopt.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Take Them Home">
    Click "Take Me Home," get in your car, and drive back to your house to place the child.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Build Care Stations">
    Place a crib and build a formula station so you can feed and care for your new kid right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Watch the Fees">
Adoption fees increase with every child you already have. Your first baby is the cheapest you will ever pay, so set aside savings before heading back for kid number two and beyond.
</Callout>

</Section>

The tutorial itself pays out. Completing the opening sequence rewards coins and unlocks your understanding of the care loop, and task lists keep feeding you objectives afterward. When you visit stores like the furniture shop, keep a budget in mind. Early on you may only have around 20 coins, so prioritize a toy bin over decorative items.

| Income Source | Effort | Speed | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hourly Coins | None | Slow | Automatic drop, collect each hour |
| Daily Login | Very low | Daily | Rewards scale with streaks |

<Section icon={Home} title="Caring for Your Kids and Upgrading Your House">

Once home, your family needs constant attention. Babies get hungry, want toys, and need cuddles. The formula station prepares bottles on a timer (your next bottle may take about a minute), so keep the cycle running rather than waiting idle.

| Care Action | How to Do It | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Feeding | Grab a bottle from the formula station | Fills hunger meter |
| Playing | Provide toys from a toy bin | Keeps kids happy |
| Cuddling | Interact with the baby directly | Boosts mood |
| Dressing | Change outfits at home | Completes tasks, earns cash |
| Sleeping | Place baby in their own crib | Restores the child |

Your house is your capacity limit. Bigger houses with yards allow you to adopt more children, which is the only way to actually raise the biggest family. Prioritize housing upgrades over cosmetic purchases whenever your budget forces a choice.

<Callout type="tip" title="Waiting Is the Game">
Lines at the adoption center, bottle prep timers, and store checkouts all involve waiting. Use that downtime to collect coins, check tasks, or plan your next purchase instead of idling.
</Callout>

</Section>
