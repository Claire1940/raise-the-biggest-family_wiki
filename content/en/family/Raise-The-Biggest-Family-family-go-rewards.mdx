### Choosing between immediate use and saving

Immediate use is appropriate when the reward completes a clear improvement, such as meeting a requirement for the next family objective. Saving is better when the item is rare, broadly useful, or connected to an unknown future condition.

Use this decision test:

- Will spending it complete a current objective?
- Does the item have more than one possible use?
- Is another reward likely to provide the same material?
- Could the next milestone require this resource?
- Can the purchase be reversed?

If the answer is uncertain, keep the item in reserve. A reward guide should help players make informed decisions, not push them toward unnecessary spending.
